How we handle your files

A short summary of what FileFlow stores and how share links stay private.

Share links

FileFlow does not expose filenames in share URLs. Each link uses an opaque token, so a link on its own reveals nothing about the file behind it.

What we store

We store the metadata needed to serve your files — such as file size and upload time — along with minimal telemetry to keep the service running.
