How it works

Share files without extra steps.

Upload a file, get a link, and let the other person download it directly. No accounts, no folders to dig through — just the file you shared.

Pick a file, get a link

Drop a file onto the dashboard or choose it from your device. FileFlow uploads it and hands you a link right away — ready to send.

Nothing to configure

There's no setup, no permissions to manage. Every link works the same way: whoever opens it can download the file, and that's it.

01

Upload

Choose a file from the dashboard. It uploads straight away — no extra confirmation screens.

02

Share

FileFlow generates a private share link. Copy it, send it by mail, or show a QR code.

03

Download

The recipient opens the link and downloads the file directly — no sign-in required.
